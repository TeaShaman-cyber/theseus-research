R11 closes the semantic QA roadmap with an explicit non-promotion decision.

## Semble

Disposition: keep per-PR advisory.

Semble remains the strongest known-failure attention router on the frozen
fixture and is current in the verified second repository. Its scores remain
ranking evidence, not confidence or correctness.

No score threshold is promoted.

## semdup

Disposition: keep advisory with repository-local cadence.

The client proved that exact-base caching can make routine use practical after
an expensive initial seed. The research repository also showed repeated
multi-minute work on docs-only changes.

Therefore semdup may run per-PR where cache/cost is acceptable, while a
repository may reduce cadence when the witness cost materially outweighs the
signal. This is an execution-policy decision, not semantic authority.

No similarity threshold becomes a merge gate.

## Needle 3

Disposition: keep as an independent advisory trace while cost remains
acceptable.

Needle provides a genuinely different embedding geometry, but its frozen
fixture ranking is weaker than Semble and the currently pinned package is
STALE_AVAILABLE. The package remains reproducible.

Refresh only when upstream changes are worth adopting. Do not infer confidence
from its distance scores.

## Promotion result

No semantic tool receives correctness authority.

No semantic score threshold is promoted.

No semantic advisory becomes a required merge check.

No automatic remediation is authorized.

The deterministic mechanics already promoted during R8-R10 remain
non-semantic: exact source identity, artifact integrity, bounded input,
freshness state, cache provenance and machine receipts.

## Retention rule

Keep the witnesses while runner cost and operational noise do not materially
interfere with primary QA.

Future promotion, retirement, threshold changes or shared semantic policy need
fresh field evidence and a new narrow issue.

The roadmap is complete. Further semantic traces are operational evidence, not
a reason to keep extending this research line by default.
